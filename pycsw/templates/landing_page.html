The harmonization approach

From heterogeneous measurements to comparable evidence.

SoilHarmony documents the context behind measurements, identifies differences between protocols and provides transparent approaches for making soil information comparable.

01 / CONTEXT

Describe the measurement

Capture and tag sampling procedures, descriptors, protocols, units, and other contextual information needed to interpret observations.

02 / ALIGN

Identify comparability

Map terminology, classifications, units and protocols to common concepts while retaining information about the original observation.

03 / HARMONIZE

Produce reusable data

Apply documented transformations and uncertainty information so harmonized data can support research, monitoring and soil-health assessment.

Featured research

Why harmonized soil data matters

Soil observations are collected using diverse sampling designs, laboratory protocols and classification systems. Without accounting for these differences, combining datasets can introduce systematic inconsistencies.

SoilHarmony develops transparent approaches for integrating these observations while preserving their provenance and uncertainty.

About SoilHarmony

Building a common language for soil information.

Soil-Harmony is an academic research project focused on improving the comparability and interoperability of soil data. The project brings together researchers, data scientists and soil experts to develop transparent approaches for harmonizing heterogeneous observations.
